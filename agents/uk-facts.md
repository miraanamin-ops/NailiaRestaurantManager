# UK hospitality facts

General facts the owner can be told when they ask (wages, VAT, working time). Each is
a fact, not advice. Last checked: 9 November 2026 (GOV.UK). Update this file when the
rates change each April.

## Minimum wage (from 1 April 2026)
- National Living Wage, age 21 and over: £12.71 an hour.
- Age 18 to 20: £10.85 an hour.
- Age 16 to 17: £8.00 an hour.
- Apprentice rate: £8.00 an hour.
- Accommodation offset: £11.10 a day.
- Already announced for 1 April 2027: £13.21 (21 and over), £11.27 (18 to 20), £8.31 (16 to 17 and apprentices).
- Source: GOV.UK, "The National Minimum Wage in 2026".

## VAT
- Standard rate: 20%. Restaurant food and drink is standard-rated (takeaway cold food can be zero-rated).
- A figure "including VAT" divided by 1.2 is the figure before VAT.

## Working time
- Most workers can't be made to work more than 48 hours a week on average (they can opt out in writing).
- 11 hours' rest between working days; a 20-minute break when working more than 6 hours.
- Paid holiday: 5.6 weeks a year for someone working 5 days a week (28 days, which can include bank holidays).

## Where to get advice
- Tax and VAT: an accountant, or HMRC.
- Employment decisions about a specific person (dismissal, disciplinary, contracts): Acas (acas.org.uk, free helpline) or an employment adviser.
- Loans and finance: an accountant or a regulated adviser.
