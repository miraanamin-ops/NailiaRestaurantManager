# DinerAI help guide

What DinerAI does, how to do each thing in the DinerAI app and on the web, what isn't possible
yet, and the answers to common questions. Written for restaurant owners.

How this guide is used: when you ask "how do I...?" in the app's Chat, the answer comes from
this guide and from your own setup. If the guide doesn't cover your question, we say so
and ask the DinerAI team for you.

---

## The basics

DinerAI is your restaurant's assistant. You run it from the DinerAI app on your phone; the
website at **app.dinerai.co.uk** is there when you want the bigger picture. Everything
in this guide that says "send" or "say" means in the app's **Chat** tab.

- **You don't need commands.** Write the way you'd talk to a manager: "Thursday looks
  quiet", "post about our new lamb ribs", "how did last week go?", "change Friday hours
  to 11pm". Typos are fine.
- **We do the work first.** Review replies, Google posts, email offers and rotas arrive
  already written. You tap **Approve**, **Edit** or **Skip** (or type 1, 2 or 3).
- **Nothing reaches your customers or Google until you approve it**, and every message is
  checked first (facts, prices, your discount cap, consent and unsubscribe links).
- **One message in the morning.** Routine things wait for your morning brief. Only urgent
  things (a 1 to 3 star review, or something you asked for) arrive straight away.
- **You can always take things back.** Send **UNDO** to reverse your last approval, skip,
  edit or setting change, where possible. Send **PAUSE** to stop all sending, **RESUME**
  to start again.

## The DinerAI app

DinerAI runs in its own app for iPhone and Android. It's on its way to the App Store and
Google Play; log in with the same email and password as on the website. Your brief and
urgent alerts arrive as notifications.

- **Chat tab**: talk to DinerAI in your own words ("Thursday is quiet"), use commands like
  PAUSE, UNDO or STATUS, and answer questions. Choices come as buttons to tap, and drafts come as cards with
  Approve, Edit and Skip.
- **Photos and files in Chat**: the camera button for a photo of a dish (it becomes a Google
  post) or of your till report (it's read in as that day's sales); the photo button to pick
  one you've taken; the paperclip for files (a menu PDF, a till export as CSV or Excel, or
  our spreadsheets filled in). Photos up to 5 MB, files up to 16 MB.
- **Inbox tab**: everything waiting for you, in four colour-coded groups, each split by
  type of message: **Urgent** (your rota, questions from DinerAI, "which did you mean?"),
  **Needs a look** (replies to 1 to 3 star reviews, review questions, email offers,
  welcome emails for an imported list, hours and menu updates, bank holiday hours,
  Google profile fixes, facts to save, and anything a check flagged), **Not urgent**
  (Google posts, special ideas) and **Simple** (replies to 4 and 5 star reviews,
  birthday emails). On a closed item, the tick approves it straight away (simple and not
  urgent ones), the eye opens it to read first (urgent and needs a look), and the pencil
  asks what to change; the arrow at the bottom opens it in full with **Approve**,
  **Edit** and **Delete** (the app's word for Skip). **Approve all** at the top takes
  only simple items whose checks all passed. After Approve or Delete you have 5 seconds
  to tap **Undo**; **Undo** at the top reverses your last decision. After a Delete you
  can tap why it didn't work (optional). A question for you has an **Answer in Chat**
  button. On the **Today** tab, **Waiting for you** shows one tile per type with its
  count: tap a tile to open the Inbox at that type.
- **Signing up in the app**: tap **Create an account** (your name, the restaurant's name,
  email, a password, your mobile, and tick the terms), or **Continue with Apple** /
  **Continue with Google**. Got a recommendation or invite code? Tap **I have a code**.
- **Paying in the app**: **Start your free month** uses your App Store or Google Play
  account: the first month is free, then it renews monthly at the price shown, until you
  cancel. Cancel or change it in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings > your name >
  Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions), or from **More >
  Manage in the App Store / Google Play**. On Android, have the FOUNDER code? Tap **I
  have an offer code** (there's no offer code on iPhone: the free month, then the monthly
  price). Signed up on the website? You keep paying there (Manage billing in settings).
- **Setup with Dina in the app**: the same setup as on the website, chapter by chapter.
  Tap **Chapters** to jump to one; take photos of your menu with the camera button. At the
  end you're asked to turn on alerts (your morning brief, and anything urgent).
- **Notifications**: your brief and alerts come to the app (a notification, and the
  message in Chat). Choose which ones you get in **More > Settings**.
- **Delete your account**: More > **Delete account**, then type the restaurant's name.
  Sending stops at once; you have 24 hours to tap **Keep my account**; then we email you a
  copy of your data and delete everything. It doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play
  subscription: cancel that in your phone's settings too.
- **Today tab**: yesterday's takings, what's waiting, and **Who's on today** from this
  week's rota.
- **Reports tab**: three sections at the top: **Weekly**, **Monthly** and **Rota**. Rota shows
  this week and next: who works when, the hours, the staff cost and how it compares with
  your labour target. A suggested rota says **Review in Inbox** (approve it there); an
  approved one has **Share with your team** (their link has no pay or costs). To change
  it, say so in Chat ("Ali can't do Friday").
- **Weekly and monthly reports**: your weekly report (every Monday) and monthly report
  (early each month), with charts. Tap **Start in Chat** on one of the 3 next actions to get it going, or
  **Share the PDF** to send it to someone.
- **More tab**: **Customers** (your list, search, your sign-up QR code, **Print the QR pack**,
  **Import a list in Chat**, **Download my list (CSV)**); **Settings** (opening hours, offers
  and the discount cap, how replies sound, your brief time, sending hours, quiet hours, and
  which notifications you get); **Recommend a restaurant** (shares your code); **Help**
  (this guide, and **Talk to a person** by email); and the **Sending is on** switch to pause
  everything at once. Changes are in your activity log, and UNDO in Chat puts the last one
  back.
- **Not yet in the app**: the activity log, the sales page, adding or editing your team
  (say it in Chat, or use the web app's Team page), and Google settings.

## Getting started (setup with Dina)

Dina takes you through setup, in the DinerAI app or on the website (app.dinerai.co.uk/onboarding). You can switch between them at any step without losing progress.
Every step can be skipped and finished later from **Settings** (**Your setup, chapter by
chapter**).

The chapters, in order:

1. **You and your restaurant** (Hello): your name, your restaurant and your website.
2. **Google** (Google profile): we find your restaurant on Google Maps, then you either tap
   **Connect Google** and sign in with the Google account you manage your profile with
   (quickest), or add DinerAI as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, with
   step-by-step pictures (if signing in doesn't work for your account). Either way we copy
   your address, hours, phone, rating, photos and reviews across.
3. **Your look and voice** (Your look, Your tone): your logo and colours (we can pick them
   up from your website, or send a photo), and the tone you want replies and posts to
   have. We show you replies to your own real reviews in four tones; pick one or write
   your own, plus your sign-off name and whether to use emoji.
4. **Your menu and margins** (Your menu): send photos or a PDF of your menu. We read it
   into sections, dishes and prices for you to check. Allergens are suggested dish by
   dish but never shown to customers until you confirm each one. You can add what each
   dish costs you (exact, estimated, or "30% for the rest") so we can check profit.
5. **Offers and limits** (Rewards and limits): your sign-up reward for new customers, the
   kinds of offer you're happy to run, the biggest discount allowed, and the minimum
   profit to keep after an offer (we recommend 50%).
6. **Customers and complaints** (Making it right, Hours and common questions,
   Customer list and QR codes): what you're happy to offer an unhappy customer and who they should
   contact; your opening hours and answers to common customer questions (halal, parking,
   bookings...); your printable QR codes; and, if you have one, your existing customer
   list (see "Importing a customer list").
7. **Sales and team** (Sales history, Team and labour): last year's till export (CSV or
   Excel, up to 25 MB) or our daily takings spreadsheet; then, if you have them, your rotas
   from the last 2 to 4 weeks (photos of the paper rota, PDFs, or your rota app's export),
   which we check with you and learn from; who works for you (main role, anything else
   they can cover, pay, hours, usual days, who can open and lock up); how many people you
   need on shift (filled in for you from your rotas, or from what restaurants your size
   usually need: change any number with − and +); and your labour cost target.
8. **Your calendar** (Languages and key dates): your customers' languages and the dates
   that matter to you.
9. **Working together** (Approvals and timing): what time your morning message arrives
   (7 to 11am) and the hours customer emails may go out.

At the end you get a summary of everything saved, with "Change" links and a list of
anything still to do. Then we run a first **business review** of your restaurant (see
below).

## Your morning brief and alerts

- **Morning brief**: at the time you chose, one message listing everything waiting (up to
  5 items, urgent first), each with Approve / Edit / Skip, plus a "done for you" line,
  today's forecast, the next few days' events and, on Mondays, labour against your
  target. If yesterday's sales are missing, it asks for them. On a quiet day you get
  nothing at all.
- **Approving several at once**: **Approve all** in the Inbox approves the simple ones whose
  checks passed, and **APPROVE ALL** in Chat approves everything in the latest brief.
  **APPROVE 2**, **EDIT 2** or **SKIP 2** acts on one numbered item. Or just say "approve
  the first two".
- **QUEUE** shows drafts waiting for you; **NEXT** brings up the next one.
- **Urgent alerts**: a 1 to 3 star review arrives straight away with a reply already
  drafted. At most one alert an hour, and none during your quiet hours (11pm to 9am unless
  you changed them).
- **Notifications**: the brief and alerts arrive as notifications from the DinerAI app.
  Turn them on when the app asks (or in your phone's settings), and choose which ones you
  get in **More > Settings**.

## Approve, Edit, Skip

- **Approve** sends or publishes it (within your sending hours).
- **Edit**: tell us what to change in your own words ("make it shorter", "say Friday not
  Thursday", "take out the discount") and we redraft it. You can also just type the change
  without tapping Edit.
- **Skip** drops it. You can tap a reason (wrong tone, wrong offer, not now); we learn
  your style from your edits and skips.
- On the web: **Approvals** shows everything waiting and your recent decisions, with the
  same Approve / Skip.

## Customers and your email list

### How customers join
- Customers scan your **QR code** (on table cards, a counter sign or a poster), fill in a
  short form in your colours, tick a box to hear from you, and confirm their email.
- After confirming they get a welcome email with your **sign-up reward**. At the till they
  tap **Redeem now**: the screen turns green with a live clock for 10 minutes, then shows
  "Already used". You get a message in the app when a reward is used.
- **Get your QR codes**: send **QR** in Chat for your sign-up link and printable QR
  pack (four table cards, a counter sign and an A4/A5 poster in your colours). On the web:
  **Customers**, then **Print QR table cards**.
- **Change your sign-up reward**: just say "set sign-up reward to a free mango lassi", or
  **Settings**, then **Offers and sending**, then **Sign-up reward**. **REWARD** shows the
  current one.

### Your customer list
- On the web: **Customers** shows everyone on your list, with search and birthdays this
  week. Your list belongs to you.
- **Export** it any time: send **EXPORT CUSTOMERS** for a download link (valid for an
  hour), or **Customers**, then **Export** (a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or
  Google Sheets). Every export is recorded in your activity log.

### Importing a customer list
You can add customers you already have (from a booking system, a loyalty scheme or a
spreadsheet), up to 5,000 at a time.

- **In the app**: send the file (CSV or Excel) in Chat with the paperclip, or **More >
  Customers > Import a list in Chat**. We read it and come back with what we found.
- **On the web**: **Customers**, then **Import customers**, then choose the file.
- We work out which columns are the name, email, phone and birthday, check every email,
  and leave out duplicates, anyone already on your list, and anyone who unsubscribed or
  asked for their data to be deleted before. You see a preview with the numbers before
  anything is saved.
- **You tell us how these people agreed to hear from you**:
  - they **opted in to marketing** (for example a tick box on your booking system), or
  - they're **past customers** who bought from you and were given a chance to opt out
    when their details were taken.
  - If you **don't know**, we can't import them. Even a "please confirm" email counts as
    marketing under UK law, so it needs one of the two reasons above too. Instead, put
    your QR codes out so they can join themselves.
- You confirm that this is true (you're responsible for having their permission). We keep
  a record of what you chose, when, and which file it came from.
- **First contact**: each imported customer gets one welcome email first, which you
  approve: whose list they're on, why, your sign-up reward, and a one-click unsubscribe.
  No offer can reach them until that welcome has gone. If you prefer, they can be asked to
  confirm their email first (only those who confirm are added); just say so when you
  import.
- Welcome emails go out in small batches each day so your emails keep landing in inboxes.
  If too many bounce (more than 5%), we pause that import and let you know the list looks
  out of date.
- **Changed your mind?** Send **UNDO** straight after, or remove the whole import in one
  go from **Customers**, then **Imports**. The import history shows the date, number of
  people, the reason you gave, emails sent, bounced and unsubscribed.

### Email offers (campaigns)
- **Ask for one in your own words**: "Thursday looks quiet", "do something for half
  term", "an offer for Eid". We draft a branded email with the offer, the audience and
  the send time, and send you a picture of it to approve. If you don't say what offer,
  we pick one from the kinds you're happy to run, within your discount cap.
- **Who it goes to**: everyone on your list, customers with a birthday in the next 7 days,
  or new sign-ups who haven't used their reward yet.
- **Rules that protect you**: only customers who agreed and confirmed; at most one offer
  per customer per week; never over your discount cap or below your minimum profit per
  dish; only within your sending hours; Unsubscribe and "Delete my data" in every email.
  Emails come from your restaurant's name, and replies go to your email.
- **Birthday emails**: every Monday we draft that week's birthday email with your
  birthday offer. **BIRTHDAY CAMPAIGN** drafts it now.
- **Quiet-day offers**: when the forecast shows a noticeably quiet day 3 to 7 days away,
  your brief has a "Draft an offer" button.
- **Results**: **CAMPAIGN RESULTS** shows how your latest campaign did (sent, opened,
  clicked, redeemed).
- **Your own copy**: **MY EMAIL you@example.com** sets where your copy of each campaign
  email goes.
- **See the automatic emails**: **EMAIL PREVIEWS** (the confirm, welcome and "how was your
  visit?" emails). On the web: **Settings**, then **How you sound**, then **How your
  emails look**, where you can also change your logo and colours.
- **Biggest discount**: **CAP 25** sets it to 25% (or **Settings**, **Offers and
  sending**).
- **"How was your visit?"**: about 3 hours after a customer uses a reward or offer, they
  get a short feedback email with your Google review link (always, whatever their rating)
  and a private form. 1 to 3 star feedback reaches you straight away. At most one per
  customer every 30 days.
- **Not yet**: offers to customers by text message (SMS) or WhatsApp. Customer offers
  are email only for now.

## Reviews (Google)

- With Google connected, we check for new reviews every hour. 4 to 5 stars: a reply is
  drafted for your morning brief. 1 to 3 stars: an alert straight away with a careful
  reply ready.
- Replies use your tone, your sign-off and your facts (hours, halal statement, how you
  make things right). We never invent facts or prices.
- **Sensitive reviews** (food poisoning, allergies, named staff, suspected fake reviews)
  come to you as a question first: reply publicly, handle it privately, or both.
- **A review from somewhere else** (TripAdvisor, Deliveroo, a message): paste it here and
  you get a reply to copy and paste.
- Ask "which reviews still need a reply?" for the list.
- On the web: **Reviews** (under **More** on a phone), with filters for low ratings and
  unanswered.
- After you approve, the reply goes onto Google. **UNDO** deletes a reply we posted.

## Google profile

- **Connect Google**: during setup (the Google chapter), or **Settings**, then
  **Connections**, then **Google Business Profile**, then **Connect Google** and sign in
  with the Google account you manage your profile with. If that doesn't work for your
  account, add DinerAI as a Manager instead (the same page shows how). You stay the owner
  of your profile either way.
- **Posts**: two drafted Google posts a week (Monday and Thursday). Send a **photo** of a
  dish (add a note if you like) and it becomes a captioned post to approve. A photo that
  won't work (too dark, blurry, a customer's face) gets a reason instead.
- **Hours**: "we close at 11 on Fridays now" becomes a card to confirm; after Approve it
  updates your settings and Google. Four weeks before a bank holiday we ask whether your
  hours are changing (Same / Closed / Different) and set special hours on Google.
- **Not yet**: sending your menu to Google (menu changes update DinerAI only).

## Sales and till reports

Your sales let us forecast quiet days, plan offers before they happen and find you more
profit. Send whatever you have; the more detail, the better:

1. **Best: your till's sales-by-item export** (CSV or Excel). Send it here or upload it on
   the web: **Sales**, then **Upload a sales file**. The first file of a new layout is
   checked with you once; after that it imports straight away.
2. **Sales by the hour**, if your till can export it.
3. **A photo of your end-of-day till report (Z-report)**. We read it, check the figures
   add up, and save it (or ask you if something looks off).
4. **Just type it**: "we took £2,340, 180 orders", "yesterday 1500 before VAT", "2.3k on
   Saturday". We show it back and save it when you say yes. Tell us if it was before or
   including VAT, or for a different day, in any words.

- **SALES** shows the last 7 days and the link to your Sales page.
- **Staff can send sales too**: **ADD STAFF +447700900123** lets a staff phone send till
  reports and sales files (nothing else). **STAFF** lists them; **REMOVE STAFF** removes
  one.
- **Spreadsheets**: send **SPREADSHEETS** for our templates (menu costs, team, daily
  takings, and an example till export), or **Settings**, then **Connections**, then
  **Spreadsheets**. Fill them in and send them back here or upload them.
- **Not yet**: a direct live connection to your till (Square, Epos Now, Lightspeed,
  Toast). It's built and waiting for the till companies' approval. Until then, send
  exports or till report photos.

## Forecasts and the week ahead

- Once you've sent about 8 weeks of sales, we forecast each day's likely takings and
  orders with a range, and the main reasons ("West Ham at home"). It's on your **Home**
  page, in your brief and in the Monday report.
- Ask "how busy will Saturday be?" or "why is Friday quiet?".
- Your local calendar is built in: bank holidays, Ramadan, Eid, Diwali, Mother's Day,
  paydays, school holidays, big football matches, local events and transport strikes.

## Team and rota

- **Your team**: tell us in your own words ("add Ali, kitchen porter, £12.50, weekdays",
  "Sara is a server who can also do the bar", "Mo can open and lock up", "Sara's going up
  to 13 quid", "Bilal's left") or use the **Team** page (**More**, then **Team and rota**; **Team** in the side menu on a computer). **TEAM**
  lists everyone.
- **Roles**: manager (or supervisor), head chef, chef (cook, sous chef, grill, tandoor),
  kitchen porter (KP, dishwasher), server (waiter or waitress), host, bar (or barista),
  runner, counter (takeaway counter, till, phones) and driver (your own drivers, not
  delivery app riders). Each person has a main role and can have others they can cover:
  the rota only uses someone outside their main role when nobody whose job it is is free,
  and says so. A head chef counts as a chef. Older teams set up as just "kitchen" and
  "front of house" keep working as before. A role of your own (e.g. "shisha") is kept as
  you wrote it.
- **How many people you need**: for each role, the fewest on whenever you're open, and
  one more for every so many orders an hour (servers count eat-in orders, counter staff
  takeaway orders, drivers delivery orders, the kitchen all orders), plus how long before
  opening each role starts and how long after closing it stays (e.g. chefs 30 minutes
  early, the porter 30 minutes after). Change it on the **Team** page under **Who you need
  on shift**; **Use these numbers** there puts in our suggestion for your size (from the
  seats you gave at setup) and the roles on your team.
- **Opening and locking up**: tick **Can open up and lock up** for anyone with keys (Team
  page, or say "Mo can open and lock up"). The rota then puts one of them on the first and
  last shift of every day, and warns you on a day it can't. Switch it off under **Who you
  need on shift** if you don't want it.
- **Your past rotas**: sent at setup, they're kept as past weeks so the rota knows who
  usually works which day from the start. Not yet possible: sending past rotas after
  setup; the rota learns from the ones it makes with you instead.
- **Rota**: every Thursday at 10am we suggest next week's rota from your opening hours,
  the forecast and your rules, with labour cost against forecast sales and warnings (over
  48 hours, too little rest). **ROTA** shows it now. Change it in plain words ("Ali can't
  do Friday", "swap Sara and Bilal on Sunday"). After Approve you get a rota page and a PDF
  to forward to staff (without pay or costs).
- **Labour target**: **LABOUR TARGET 28%** sets it (or at setup, where we show what your
  past rotas ran at); your brief and reports check the rota against it.

## Menu, prices and profit

- Change your menu in plain words ("add lamb chops, £14", "lamb chops are £15 now",
  "take the soup off") or **Settings**, then **Your restaurant**, then **Menu**.
- **Allergens**: **Settings**, then **Allergens**. Unconfirmed allergens are never shown
  to customers or used in messages.
- **Dish costs and profit**: add what each dish costs you (in setup, the menu costs
  spreadsheet from **SPREADSHEETS**, or settings). With costs and item sales we show profit
  per dish and group dishes into winners, crowd-pleasers, hidden gems and ones to rethink,
  in your Monday report. No offer can leave a costed dish below your minimum profit.
- **Ideas and advice**: ask "what are three ways to improve my margins?" or "how can we
  grow revenue next year?". You get ideas based on your own numbers, each with a next step
  we can do for you. We give information about your business, not financial, tax or legal
  advice.

- **Monthly report**: early each month, last month like for like, busiest days and hours,

## Your data and privacy

- **Your customer list is yours.** Export it any time (EXPORT CUSTOMERS).
- **Customers control their data**: every email has Unsubscribe and "Delete my data".
  Deleting removes their personal details; we only keep anonymous counts. Anyone who
  unsubscribes or deletes their data is never added back, even by an import.
- We never share your data with other restaurants. The nearby comparison in the monthly
  report only uses averages across 5 or more restaurants, and you can switch it off in
  **Settings**, then **Customers and account**, then **Nearby comparison**.
- The AI never sees your customers' contact details.

## Logging in, plan and billing

- **Log in** at app.dinerai.co.uk with your email and password. Forgot it? Tap **Forgot your
  password?** on the login page and we email you a link to set a new one.
- **Plan**: £99 a month. **Settings**, then **Customers and account**, then **Plan and
  billing** shows your plan. During the pilot, billing questions go to the DinerAI team
  and we'll come back to you.
- **Free trial**: your first month is free. Your card is taken at sign-up and the first £99
  is charged on the same date the next month. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.
- **Recommend a restaurant**: send **REFER** (or see **Recommend a restaurant** in
  Settings) for your code and link. When a restaurant signs up with it and pays its first
  month, you get a month free (£99 off your next bill). There's no limit.
- **Closing your account**: ask us and we'll send you a full export of your data, then
  delete everything (after a 24-hour cooling-off period).

## Common questions

**Can you text or WhatsApp my customers?** Not yet. Customer messages are email only for
now.

**Do I need WhatsApp?** No. DinerAI runs in its own app: your brief, alerts, approvals
and chat are all there, with notifications.

**Can I connect Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or other accounting software?** Not yet. DinerAI
doesn't connect to accounting software. For your sales, send your till's export (CSV or
Excel), a photo of the end-of-day till report, or just type the day's takings ("we took
£2,340"). For costs, use the menu costs spreadsheet (send **SPREADSHEETS**). If you'd like
an accounting connection, say so and we'll pass it on to the DinerAI team.

**Can you connect to my till (Square, Epos Now, Lightspeed, Toast)?** Not yet: a direct
connection is built and waiting for the till companies' approval. Until then, send your
till's export or a photo of the end-of-day report (see **Sales and till reports**).

**Can you connect to Deliveroo, Uber Eats or Just Eat?** Not yet. You can include their
sales in your takings by sending your till export or typing the day's total. To reply to
a review from one of them, paste it in Chat and we write a reply for you to copy.

**Can you connect to my booking system (OpenTable, ResDiary, SevenRooms) or Mailchimp?**
Not directly. You can import a customer list exported from them: **More > Customers >
Import a list in Chat**, or **Customers > Import customers** on the website.

**Can you post on Instagram, Facebook or TikTok?** Not yet. We draft Google posts. We can
write a caption for you to post yourself: just ask.

**Can you take bookings or orders?** No. DinerAI works alongside your booking and till
systems.

**Can I have more than one restaurant?** Each restaurant has its own account for now.

**Can someone else approve things?** Only you, logged in to your account, can approve.
Staff phones can send sales only.

**Why didn't my offer go out yet?** Emails only go out within your sending hours (9am to
9pm unless you changed them), never while paused, and each customer gets at most one offer
a week.

**Why can't I import my list from my till or booking system without saying how they
agreed?** UK marketing law needs a reason for every email we send on your behalf, even a
welcome. Choosing the reason and confirming it keeps you and your customers protected.

**Something's wrong or I need a person**: just say so here, and the DinerAI team will get
back to you.
