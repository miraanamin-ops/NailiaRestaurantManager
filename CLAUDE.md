# Naila (working name)

## What this is
An AI marketing assistant for independent single-site restaurants. The owner runs everything through WhatsApp: the AI drafts the work, the owner taps Approve, Edit or Skip, and nothing goes out unless it passes plain-code safety rules. The user is not a developer, so explain things simply and give exact click paths when you need something from them.

Right now it's being built to test alone with dummy data. No real customers, no live Google connection.

## Stack
Next.js (TypeScript) on Vercel, Supabase (database), GitHub, Claude API (Sonnet), Twilio WhatsApp sandbox (owner channel only), Resend (customer emails from our verified domain mail.dinerai.co.uk, EU region; React Email templates). All keys are Vercel environment variables, never in code.

## Built so far
1. Skeleton app with dummy data: one restaurant (halal grill in East London), customers and Google-style reviews, plus a page showing the data.
2. WhatsApp assistant: the owner chats with Claude, which has the restaurant profile and data as context. All messages logged.
3. Approval loop: drafts sent with Approve, Edit, Skip. Edits and skip reasons saved and used to learn the restaurant's tone. "NEW REVIEW" test command.
4. Safety rules in code: nothing sends without approval, 9am to 9pm UK send window, discount cap per restaurant, no duplicate sends, PAUSE/RESUME, blocked sends logged. A separate Claude checker reviews each draft. "TIME hh:mm" test command.
5. Customer sign-up by email (not WhatsApp): QR code to a branded form, unticked consent box, consent saved with timestamp and wording, privacy notice. Owner sets the sign-up reward via WhatsApp. Welcome email with a one-time redemption page: "Redeem now" turns the screen green with a live clock, expires after 10 minutes, then shows "Already used". Owner notified on WhatsApp. Unsubscribe link in every email.
6. Email campaigns: owner texts e.g. "Thursday is quiet", gets a drafted offer to approve, sent to a segment (everyone, birthdays in next 7 days, unredeemed new sign-ups). Weekly birthday draft. Only consented customers. Real email to the owner, dummy customers marked "simulated sent". Tracks sends, opens, clicks, redemptions.
7. Google: all actions behind a "Google connector" module with dummy and live modes. Hourly review check: 4 to 5 stars to the approval queue (held for the morning brief since step 8), 1 to 3 stars trigger an instant alert. Pasted real reviews get a copy-paste reply. 2 drafted posts a week, WhatsApp photos captioned into posts. "RUN REVIEWS" and "RUN POSTS" test commands. Google-style preview page.
8. Morning brief and Monday report: drafts the system makes on its own (review replies, posts, birthday emails) are held quietly; only 1 to 3 star alerts and things the owner asks for arrive straight away. 9am brief: numbered list of everything waiting, each item with its own Approve/Edit/Skip buttons, "APPROVE ALL" (or "APPROVE 2" etc.), plus a one-line "done for you" tally; tally only if nothing to approve, nothing at all on a quiet day. If the owner hasn't messaged in 24 hours (WhatsApp rule) the brief waits and arrives with their next message. Monday report replaces the Sunday round-up: mobile-first web page (headline, reputation, customers and campaigns, Google visibility, next week's 3 actions with "Start in WhatsApp" buttons), last week vs the week before with small charts, modular sections, "Download PDF" with the same content, two-line WhatsApp headline with the link. "RUN BRIEF" and "RUN REPORT" test commands. Two weeks of removable dummy activity (`scripts/seed-two-weeks.mjs`).
9. Hardening and tidy-up: only the registered owner WhatsApp number can use the assistant (others ignored); duplicate Twilio deliveries ignored; test-data dashboard behind a password; the morning job retries each step hourly and tells the owner if it fails; scheduled jobs always use the real clock; all Claude calls on Sonnet 5.5 with refusal fallback; `bot.ts` split into `lib/bot/`; shared date/text helpers; `setup.sql` can't wipe an existing database; every SQL file records itself in `schema_migrations`. "REPORT" renamed "CAMPAIGN RESULTS".
10. 11. Multi-restaurant support: each restaurant is identified by its owner's WhatsApp number; unknown numbers get a polite "not registered" reply (at most once a day) and nothing else. Every query is filtered by restaurant, nothing picks "the first restaurant", a restaurant can only send its own drafts, and scheduled jobs run every active restaurant separately so one failing doesn't stop the others. Logged-in pages show only the owner's restaurant (the builder can switch). A second demo restaurant, Cardamom Corner Café in Ilford (own menu, voice, reward, customers, reviews), run from a second phone. Isolation tests prove one restaurant can't see or send to another's customers, drafts or reviews.
12. Onboarding new restaurants: a sign-up page (owner name, email, restaurant name, WhatsApp number) and magic-link login, then a choice: set up in the browser or on WhatsApp (a button and QR code open WhatsApp with a one-time link code; in sandbox mode it explains joining the sandbox first). One engine with saved progress behind both, so the owner can switch at any step. Six steps, each skippable: find the restaurant on Google ("Is this you?", then address, hours, phone, rating and photos are copied), menu photos read into dishes, prices and sections to check (an editable table on the web), allergens suggested per dish but never shown to customers until the owner confirms, three sample messages from the website (and optional Instagram captions) to pick a voice, a sign-up reward and discount cap ready to accept in one tap, then finish: the WhatsApp number must be linked, a printable QR pack (four table cards and a counter sign in the restaurant's colours) and a summary on WhatsApp and the web. Afterwards anything can be changed by message ("change Friday hours to 11pm", "add lamb chops, £14") or on a settings page, and UNDO puts it back. "RESET ONBOARDING" test command.

Real, trustworthy customer email: sent from our verified domain as the restaurant ("Ember & Spice Grill <hello@mail.dinerai.co.uk>"), reply-to the restaurant's email; dummy addresses are never emailed and TEST_MODE redirects everyone except the builder and owner to the builder. Double opt-in: a "confirm your email" message first; consent and the reward only become active once they tap it. Sign-up form protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and rate limits. Every email footer has Unsubscribe and "Delete my data" (personal details removed, anonymous counts kept). The owner can export their customer list as CSV from settings or by texting "EXPORT CUSTOMERS". Deletions and exports are in the audit log. "RUN FEEDBACK" test command.

Domains and loose ends: the app runs on app.dinerai.co.uk and every link we make (emails, QR codes, rewards, reports, login links) uses it through the one APP_URL setting; old vercel.app links redirect there. dinerai.co.uk shows a one-page holding site (what DinerAI does, contact email, privacy notice and terms), for owners and Meta business verification. WhatsApp link previews no longer count as customer-list downloads (a Download button), so one export is one line in the log.

Sales data without a POS integration (a photo or a file, never typing numbers): the owner, or a staff number they register ("ADD STAFF +447..."; staff can only send data), sends a photo of the end-of-day till report (Z-report). Claude reads it (date, gross/net, VAT, sales count, card/cash/other, discounts, refunds, hourly if shown); code checks it adds up and saves it with one line, figures to the penny in till order ("Thu 8 Oct: £2,808.00 sales, £468.00 VAT, £2,340.00 net, 118 transactions. Missing yesterday's report adds a one-line nudge to the morning brief. Daily weather per restaurant (Open-Meteo) with a 12-month backfill. A Sales section in the Monday report (only when there's data): net sales vs last week and the same week last month, by-day chart, average spend, best/worst day, top 5 items, "not financial advice" footnote. 12 months of dummy sales with built-in patterns (`scripts/seed-sales.mjs`), 3 sample till report photos plus a bad one and a sample POS CSV (`public/samples/`), and test commands SAMPLE ZREPORT, TEST ZREPORT and RUN WEATHER.
16. Agent organisation, phase 1 (foundation and checker, shadow mode): the design files in `/agents` (runtime prompts) and `docs/agent-system.md`, `docs/profile-schema.md`. Agent loader (front matter, house rules + agent prompt, sonnet/haiku map, tool allow-list, files bundled into every function). Shared draft schema and a separate profile change schema, checked by code; invalid output gets one retry, then the job fails and the builder is alerted. Agent jobs with unique keys (`agent_jobs`), and the audit log records job, agent, version and verdict. Profile v1: sign-off name, greeting, complaint contact, halal and allergen statements, recovery offers, words to avoid and emoji limits on the settings page; every profile change is a version, with a Revert button per field (not UNDO); drafts record the profile version they were written with. Release gate: trust levels per task (all Approve; only the builder can set Auto or Draft only, from builder controls on the settings page; campaigns, offers, hours, menu and profile changes never Auto), TEST_MODE as a gate step, and no customer gets more than one campaign in 7 days (birthdays excepted). The checker agent runs in shadow mode on every new or edited draft: its verdict and minor fixes are recorded next to the four checks and on /log, and nothing changes for the owner. Agent tests: offline on every push, live with real Claude on demand only.
17. Agent organisation, phase 2 (Reputation): review replies move onto the agent pipeline with `agents/reputation.md`. Shadow mode (the default, per restaurant): every review the hourly check drafts, and every review the owner pastes, also goes to Reputation in the background; its reply goes through the checker loop and is saved next to today's reply for the builder's comparison on /log (Reputation comparison). Live mode (only once the go-live checklist passes: sign-off name, complaint contact, recovery offers, halal and allergen statements): Reputation writes the replies and the checker is blocking (minor fixes applied by code; a fail goes back with the fix notes, at most 2 revisions; then a question). The "needs the owner" question card (food poisoning, allergy, unverifiable claims, fake reviews, staff named, instructions in a review, or the checker can't pass a reply) arrives with Reply publicly / Handle privately / Both: privately closes it, the other two send Reputation back to write the reply with that choice in mind. A "What customers said" section in the Monday report (Reputation's weekly themes, counted by code). Builder controls: Reputation off / shadow / live with the checklist, and a WhatsApp prompt to the owner for missing fields (at most once a day). Checker 0.3 (C7: phone numbers are fine in review replies). Phone is an Identity profile field. No em-dashes in the QR pack, report PDF or report words. `npm run test:agents:stability` runs each live case 3 times and reports verdict flips. `writeReviewReply` stays until every restaurant is live on Reputation and the builder confirms.
18. Agent organisation, phase 3a (Visibility): Google posts, photos and Google updates move onto the agent pipeline with `agents/visibility.md`. Shadow mode (the default): the Monday and Thursday post, every photo the owner sends, every staff photo that isn't a till report, every change to opening hours, special hours or the menu, and the Monday content brief also go to Visibility in the background; its output goes through the checker loop (Google posts with a rendered preview, so the brand checks run) and sits next to today's on /log (one comparison tab per agent: Reputation, Visibility). Photos are checked first: unusable ones get a reason (too dark, blurry, not food, a customer's face or a child, not on the menu...) and, live, a fixed tip to the staff number that sent it. Owner and staff photos go into a photo library (`restaurants.photo_library`, seeded from the Google photos). A change to hours, special hours or the menu becomes an "hours update" or "menu update" draft showing what's on Google now and after, that always needs Approve (never Auto or Draft only, in code); approving logs what would have been sent (Google isn't connected for hours or menus yet). Each Monday, code looks 4 weeks ahead for UK bank holidays (gov.uk, cached) with no special hours set: live, a question in the brief ("Christmas Day is in 3 weeks. Are your hours changing?") with Same hours / Closed / Different hours, which saves the special hours (UNDO-able) and so brings the matching Google update. Live mode needs the go-live checklist (logo, brand colours, hours, emoji setting, 5+ photos) and, for now, TEST_MODE. Reputation now reads opening and special hours (new case: a complaint about being closed on a day the profile says it's closed). Escalation rate (questions as % of reviews, 30 days) on the Reputation comparison. Every agent job records its tokens and estimated cost; Builder controls show cost per restaurant per month by agent; agent prompts are cached. One-off writing stays with today's assistant until the GM router (phase 4).
19. Agent organisation, phase 3b (Insights): the owner's number questions and the Monday report's words move onto the agent pipeline with `agents/insights.md`. First, two Owner profile fields the GM will need: language (default English) and quiet hours (default 11pm to 9am, UK time), on a new "About you" card in settings with the owner's name (`022_owner_preferences.sql`; stored only, nothing uses quiet hours until phase 4). `query_metrics` works out every figure in plain code (sales by day, weekday, hour and item, transactions, average spend, weather, rainy against dry days, reviews, list growth, campaign results, Google stats when connected), each with its period, what it's compared with, its source, a small-sample warning (under 4 weeks of sales data, under 20 redemptions) and days that look wrong (2.5 times or under 0.4 times the usual for that weekday); covers, dish margins and labour cost say what's missing. Insights calls it as a tool (the first agent with a tool loop) and its answer must pass plain-code rules (every figure from a query, every number in the words a figure, "coincided with" not "caused", small samples flagged, an odd till figure asked about first, no staffing, pay, loan or tax advice), then the checker's owner-only checks (facts and C6, decision 3). Shadow (the default): a message that looks like a number question gets today's answer, and Insights answers in the background; each Monday report also gets Insights' headline, sales words and 3 actions around figures code prepared; both sit next to today's words on /log (Insights comparison, with the figures, queries and time taken). Live (test mode only, once there are 14+ days of sales figures from till reports or POS files; a demo's dummy data counts in test mode): Insights answers number questions (today's assistant if it can't) and writes the report's words, with a sales summary on the report page and PDF (today's words if it can't, or if it asks a question). 19 saved cases built on the demo restaurants' dummy year (`agents/tests/insights/`, fixtures from `scripts/export-insights-fixture.mjs`). Insights now reads opening and special hours. Z-report and POS reading stay as they were.
20. Agent organisation, phase 3c (Growth): campaigns move onto the agent pipeline with `agents/growth.md` (email only; WhatsApp stays blocked in the gate). New Commercial profile fields on the settings page: the kinds of offer the owner is happy to run (% off, £ off, a free item with a purchase, kids eat free, 2 for 1) and the birthday offer (`023_growth.sql`). Structured offers: a campaign can carry its offer as fields (`campaigns.offer_details`: type, dishes, the dish it comes with, % or £ value, dates, conditions) and the gate checks the discount cap from them: a free item or £ off as a % of the menu price of the dish it comes with ("with any main" = the cheapest main), 2 for 1 as 50%, blocked if code can't measure it; the wording check stays as a backstop. The owner's own birthday offer and sign-up reward aren't capped, but only when the offer's description is that offer word for word (the gate re-checks it against the restaurant at send time; the agent can't mark anything exempt). A local calendar (`local_calendar`: date, name, type, date uncertain, for every restaurant or one) filled from builder controls, with UK bank holidays from gov.uk. Growth's rules in code: a real segment with its real size, copy that fits the template, an approved kind of offer for dishes on the menu, within the cap, clear dates and conditions, a send time in sending hours on or before the offer's first day, no fake urgency, no targeting by religion, an expected outcome that quotes past results exactly or says "First campaign of this kind, no baseline yet."; the birthday batch uses the birthday offer (or asks); special ideas use menu dishes and never carry a price. Each draft goes through the checker with a picture of the email (brand checks) and a note of what the template adds. Shadow (the default): an owner's ask for an offer gets today's campaign and Growth drafts beside it; the Monday birthday email likewise; each Monday Growth also proposes the week's plan (at most 2, or nothing), drafts for calendar dates 2 to 3 weeks ahead, and suggests up to 2 special ideas (draft only); quiet times are worked out from the last 8 weeks of sales. /log has a Growth comparison with a picture of each email. Live (test mode only, once the checklist passes: cap, offer types, sign-up offer, logo, colours): Growth answers offer requests, writes the birthday batch and Monday drafts (held for the brief) as real campaigns with their offer's fields; today's flow if it can't. Special ideas stay draft only. 18 saved cases.
21. Agent organisation, phase 4 (the GM): the GM (`agents/gm.md`) sits between the owner and the specialists. First, three loose ends: the Agent tests workflow runs only when started by hand; a plain-code price check (every £ amount in a Reputation, Visibility or Growth draft must be a menu price, a value in the offer's fields or the owner's own offers); Growth's send time is used (the gate holds an approved campaign as "scheduled" and the hourly job sends it when due; `024_gm.sql`). A Haiku router reads each owner message and picks one of: reviews, Visibility, one-off writing, Insights, Growth, a fact about the restaurant, a simple profile question, general chat, or "unclear". Fixed commands (APPROVE, SKIP, RUN..., UNDO etc.) stay in code and never reach it. A Sonnet composer writes the GM's words: the brief summary, one short card per item, alerts, replies to simple profile questions (checked by the checker before sending) and the 2-line Monday message; receipts and every number come from code. Rules in code: at most 5 brief cards, urgent first, then by send date, then oldest, the rest roll to tomorrow; at most one alert an hour (a second waits for the next hour) and none in quiet hours (they go when quiet hours end, released by the hourly job, and are dropped if dealt with first); skip offers three one-tap reasons (Wrong tone / Wrong offer / Not now), never a follow-up question; an unclear message gets one question with 2 or 3 buttons; a fact about the restaurant ("we close at 11 on Fridays now") becomes a confirm card with Approve / Edit / Skip that saves through the settings flow (UNDO-able); an "On it, back in a minute" line if a reply takes over 8 seconds, at most once, in the owner's language. Scheduled work's questions (Growth's Monday questions, Insights' missing till reports) arrive in the brief as cards answered in words, and the answer goes back to that agent; Growth's special ideas arrive as Draft-only cards with Like it / Skip. General chat and look-ups stay with today's assistant for now. Shadow (the default): the router runs on every message and a shadow brief on every brief, beside today's on /log (GM comparison, with times). Live (test mode only, once the checklist passes: owner name, language, quiet hours and at least one specialist live): the GM routes, composes the brief, the Monday message and alerts; today's code answers whenever it can't. 40 routing cases and 11 composer cases.

## Design rules to keep following
- Every message to the owner carries a decision or a result. No "just checking in".
- Do the work before asking: arrive with a finished draft so approval is one tap.
- Batch, don't drip. Real-time only for urgent things.
- Nothing reaches a customer unchecked.
- Every feature gets a test command so the owner can trigger it without waiting.

## Now protected
- Only a registered owner number can use WhatsApp, and only for its own restaurant; fake (unsigned or tampered) and duplicate messages are rejected; unknown numbers get one polite reply a day.
- One restaurant can never see or send to another's customers, drafts or reviews (isolation tests).
- Owner web pages need a login; report links expire after 30 days.
- Every send passes the four checks; consent and unsubscribe links are re-checked at send time.
- Every action is logged with who and when; UNDO reverses the owner's last action where possible.
- Scheduled jobs record real outcomes and alert the builder on failure, a stuck run or lateness; no review is skipped by a timeout.
- Test commands are off unless TEST_MODE=true; dummy data can be cleared in one go.
- Claude never receives the customer list or contact details.
- Allergens are never shown to customers, or used in messages to them, until the owner confirms them.
- Customer emails come from our authenticated domain (DKIM, SPF and DMARC pass); nobody gets marketing until they confirm their email; dummy addresses are never emailed; test mode can't reach real customers.
- The sign-up form has a CAPTCHA (when its keys are set), a bot trap and rate limits; feedback, confirm and delete endpoints are rate-limited.
- Every customer email has Unsubscribe and Delete my data links; the Google review link goes to every customer, never only happy ones.
- Deletions and customer-list exports are in the audit log; export links expire after an hour.
- A WhatsApp number is only linked to a restaurant by sending that restaurant's one-time code (from its logged-in setup page); guessing is capped per number.
- Tests run on every push and before every deploy.
- Staff numbers can only send till reports and sales files for their own restaurant (one restaurant per number); they can't chat, approve or change anything.
- Sales data follows the same isolation rules and tests as everything else; till report photos and POS files are in a private bucket (signed links that last an hour); the AI only sees a few POS rows to work out the columns, with staff, customer and contact details blanked out.
- The AI only reads till reports; plain code decides whether they add up, and nothing doubtful is saved without a YES.
- A question for the owner (about a review, or a bank holiday's hours) is never sent by the gate, and APPROVE ALL leaves it for the owner's own answer.
- Changes to hours or the menu on Google always need the owner's Approve (never Auto or Draft only). Visibility can't go live outside TEST_MODE, and its staff tips are fixed wording. Reputation can only go live once its go-live checklist passes, and then the checker is blocking for its replies.
- Trust levels are per task and only the builder can change them; campaigns, offers, hours, menu and profile changes can never be Auto, and Auto needs a live checker pass on that exact version (enforced in code, not in a prompt).
- In test mode the gate itself stops anything reaching live Google; WhatsApp to customers is off until Meta verification.
- No customer gets more than one campaign in any 7 days (birthday emails excepted), checked in code at send time.
- Agents only get the tools listed in their file (an allow-list in code), never a tool that sends, publishes or spends, and only their own restaurant's data. An agent can't change a settings-only profile field (the cap, the send window).

## Known limits (to fix before real restaurants)
- Restaurants are kept apart by the code (every query filtered, enforced by tests), not by the database: the app uses Supabase's secret key, which bypasses row-level security. Adding RLS policies per restaurant would be a second, database-level wall.
- Anyone can use the restaurant sign-up page (`/start`), behind Turnstile, a hidden bot field, rate limits and at most 5 unfinished sign-ups per email per day. A new restaurant stays inactive until onboarding finishes. All restaurants share the Twilio sandbox number, and each owner must join the sandbox first.
- Onboarding's Google step needs GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY; without it the step is skipped. Menu reading and allergen suggestions are Claude's best guess: the owner checks them, and allergens are never used for customers until confirmed.
- WhatsApp sandbox: outside the 24-hour window the 9am brief waits for the owner's next message, and builder WhatsApp alerts may not arrive (needs a Meta-approved template and a real sender). Email alerts still work.
- Email: Supabase's built-in login email is rate-limited (a few per hour). Customer emails land in Gmail's Promotions tab (normal for offers). Resend's free plan: 100 emails a day, 3,000 a month. The fixed email wording comes in three tones picked from the brand voice by keywords, not written per restaurant. Preview pictures are a close drawing of the email, not a screenshot. A customer who never ticked the marketing box still gets the "How was your visit?" email (a service message, no offers) unless they unsubscribe.
- The compliance check for misleading claims is a word list: it catches common problems, not every one.
- UNDO can't take back an email already sent, or anything posted on live Google (only dummy mode).
- Tests replace the database with a stand-in; nothing yet runs against a real test database.
- Live Google connector is a stub.
- Sales: till report reading is Claude's reading of a photo (checked by code, but a misread figure that still adds up would be saved). POS files: CSV and .xlsx only (not old .xls); net is estimated at 20% VAT (food at other rates would be slightly off); sales are only counted when the file has a receipt/order number column. File layouts are learned per restaurant. Twilio's WhatsApp may not deliver every document type (CSV from some phones); the web upload always works. Weather is Open-Meteo's model data for the restaurant's postcode (via postcodes.io), not a weather station. The brief only nudges restaurants that have sent a till report in the last 14 days.
- Agents (phases 1 and 2): the checker is only blocking for Reputation's own replies (live mode); everywhere else it records in shadow mode. The old single assistant still writes every draft except review replies at restaurants where Reputation is live. Weekly themes are checked by code (the reviews exist, counts), not by the checker. The checker's verdicts aren't fully steady run to run (Sonnet 5.5 doesn't take temperature 0); the stability runs measure it. A question card's answer can't be undone with UNDO. Drafts from before the agents have no sources of their own, so code lists the profile fields each kind may rely on. Rendered previews exist for emails only (B1 to B4 are skipped for posts and replies). Only the profile fields Reputation, Visibility and the checker need are stored so far. Visibility (phase 3a): Google isn't connected for hours, menus or posts (dummy mode logs what would have been sent); live mode only works with TEST_MODE on; trend ideas and local calendar dates (religious dates, local events) wait for a trend scan and a local calendar; special hours can only be set by answering the bank holiday question (no settings field yet); a hours update can still be "edited" as text, which doesn't change the hours themselves.
- Insights (phase 3b): unless the GM is live, number questions are spotted by a word list, so some are missed and a few others are sent to it (shadow only costs money). Live mode only works in test mode. Covers aren't recorded (transactions instead); dish margins, labour cost and sales targets need data no restaurant has yet (menu engineering and rival watch are later). Google views, calls and direction requests don't exist until Google is connected. "Last week" is always the last full Monday to Sunday, so RUN REPORT mid-week covers a different week from today's report. When a week's figures are incomplete, Insights asks for the missing till reports rather than writing the report words (live: today's words are kept; the question goes in the brief when the GM is live, otherwise it's only recorded). Answers take 10 to 40 seconds (tool calls plus the checker); the "On it" line covers the wait only when the GM is live.
- Growth (phase 3c): live mode only works in test mode. A scheduled campaign goes out at the first hourly run after its send time (up to an hour late). Questions from the live Monday work, and special ideas, reach the owner only through the GM's brief (when the GM is live); otherwise they're recorded, and special ideas stay on /log.
- The GM (phase 4): live mode only works in test mode. General chat, look-ups, pasted reviews and drafts for a specialist in shadow still go to today's single assistant (its big prompt is retired only once the builder confirms). If the router is slow (over 10 seconds) or fails, today's assistant answers. Button titles set per message (the "which did you mean?" choices) haven't been tried on the real Twilio sandbox yet; typing 1, 2 or 3 always works. Allergen and halal statements can't be changed by message (settings page only). Alerts are held at most until the next hourly job, so a held alert can arrive up to an hour after it's due. Replies to simple profile questions go through the checker, which adds a few seconds. The calendar has bank holidays only until the builder adds religious dates, school holidays and local events. Free-item offers are measured against one dish's price, so "free dessert with any main" counts against the cheapest main; owners who want generous free items can raise their cap. Growth asks when details are missing (uncertain dates, reward terms, hours that don't fit), which is right but means fewer drafts until the profile is fuller.
